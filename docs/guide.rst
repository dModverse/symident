Guide
=====

The guide shows how a model is formulated, analyzed locally and globally, and reduced with
symident. Each chapter introduces the examples where they are used; the first chapters follow one running
example, a precursor that is made on a stimulus and becomes an observed product.
:doc:`method` gives the mathematics behind each step, and :doc:`api` the full signatures.
